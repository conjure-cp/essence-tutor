# Arithmetic and Comparison
Essence includes the standard arithmetic and comparison operators.

## Arithmetic
- `x + y` - addition.
- `x - y` - subtraction.
- `x * y` - multiplication.
- `x / y` - *integer* division.
- `x % y` - modulo (remainder of `x` divided by `y`).
- `x ** y` - exponential (`x` to the power of `y`).
- `x!` or `factorial(x)` - factorial.
- `|x|` - absolute value (positive value).

## Comparison
- `x = y` - equality (notice that it's one symbol!).
- `x != y` - inequality.
- `x < y` - less than.
- `x <= y` - less than or equal to.
- `x > y` - greater than.
- `x >= y` - greater than or equal to.
