# Essence
So, why use Essence?

There are a number of reasons, but they all essentially come back to making it easier to get computers to solve CSPs.

- Essence provides a standard way of describing problems; you don't have to understand (or even care about!) the ways in which different kinds of problems are solved. Instead, you tell the computer what you want to achieve and it figures it out for you.
- It is a high-level language for working with problems which you can plug into a variety of solvers. You don't need to know how to communicate with any specifically, just write your problem description in Essence and it can be translated.
