# What are constraint satisfaction problems?
Have you ever solved a [Sudoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudoku) puzzle or completed a [Nonogram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonogram)?
Then you've solved a constraint satisfaction problem (CSP)!

CSPs are puzzles in which you have a number of entities that each hold certain constraints, or requirements relating to how the world may be.
